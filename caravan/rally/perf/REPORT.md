## What the baseline showed (the evidence behind the tiers)
* 2676 draw calls and 1.57 M triangles per frame from 3203 meshes (1073 instanced scenery tiles holding 16823 instances, drawn out to 900 m), 2481 shadow casters into a 2048² map every frame, pixel ratio up to 2, fog 420–3600 m with a 9000 m far plane.
* In-page attribution on the baseline (same frame, toggles only): no shadows −31 % render time, half pixel ratio −41 %, scenery hidden −47 % (and −377 calls).
* 103 textures totalling 74 MB of texels: the same 768² files are loaded several times with different repeat settings (separate GPU uploads).

## What remains after the tiers
Even at Minimal ~1344 draw calls stay: the procedural buildings, signs, trees and cars are many small meshes each. The next step is
geometry merging per material for static objects and texture de-duplication; it needs a build-time pass over the bundle's
model builders, not a runtime toggle, and is planned as the second performance commit.
