## What the baseline showed (the evidence behind the tiers)
* 2676 draw calls and 1.57 M triangles per frame from 3203 meshes (1073 instanced scenery tiles holding 16823 instances, drawn out to 900 m), 2481 shadow casters into a 2048² map every frame, pixel ratio up to 2, fog 420–3600 m with a 9000 m far plane.
* In-page attribution on the baseline (same frame, toggles only): no shadows −31 % render time, half pixel ratio −41 %, scenery hidden −47 % (and −377 calls).
* 103 textures totalling 74 MB of texels: the same 768² files are loaded several times with different repeat settings (separate GPU uploads).

## Geometry merging (commit 2)
Composition probe before merging (High tier, 1280×720): the nine vehicles accounted for ~1 280 draw calls (130–160 meshes each),
instanced scenery 293, Knowledge-Stop gates 128, terrain tiles 111, dhows 90, checkpoints 65, road 61, fort 42. The merge
folds each vehicle's parts per material in the vehicle's own space (wheels, steering and spin groups stay separate owners) and
static meshes per 150 m cell; gates, barriers, stones, flags, camels, sea, waterfall, particles and instanced scenery are left
untouched. Texture de-duplication found 173 texture references over 100 distinct sources and nothing to free: the "duplicates"
in the first profile were different textures (generated canvases for plates and signs), not repeated file loads.

## What remains
Gates and checkpoints (~190 calls) carry their own animations and were not merged; wheels cost ~12 draws per car. A further
step would merge their static sub-parts after reading each builder, or reduce wheel materials, and is not part of this commit.
